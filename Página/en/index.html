Manufacturing & development

Development to specification and reverse engineering

We design, formulate and manufacture every component to fit its application, with no catalogue minimums. Development starts from a physical sample, a drawing or a technical specification and follows an engineering process with validation before production.

Starting point

A development can begin with or without prior documentation. The route sets the first stretch of engineering work.

Reverse engineeringfrom a physical sample

Reproduction of the component from the original part, with no drawings or specifications. It covers dimensional analysis of the sample, characterisation of the material —hardness, density, behaviour— and definition of an equivalent or improved formulation.

Development from drawingclient dimensional drawing

Tolerance review and adaptation of the geometry to the moulding and vulcanising process, with compound selection based on the stated service conditions.

Development to specificationtechnical specification

The client defines pressure, temperature, contact fluid and type of movement; the material and component design are proposed from that data, with no sample or drawing required.

Development process

1
Requirement intake

Physical sample, drawing or technical specification from the client, together with the component's operating conditions.

2
Technical review

Assessment of pressure, temperature, contact fluid, type of movement and replacement frequency, and selection of the elastomer base.

3
Design and formulation

Definition of the component geometry and of the compound —NBR, HNBR, XNBR, FKM, neoprene, polyurethane, EPDM or POLYKRAN® reinforcement—, mixed in-house.

4
Prototype

Manufacture of a pilot batch for dimensional and functional evaluation before releasing production.

5
Validation

Bench or field testing subject to availability, with iterative adjustment of material or geometry if required.

6
Production

Manufacture of the final batch by compression, transfer or injection, with dimensional and hardness control.

7
Follow-up

Field performance assessment and adjustment of the formulation or geometry on subsequent orders.

Frequently asked questions

Which sealing components do you manufacture for Oil & Gas?

Elastomeric seals for wireline and slickline (inner and outer seals, stuffing box, pack off, oil saver), workover and well control (BOP seals, BOP rams, packer elements, packer cups), flowline and pipeline (swab cups, stripper rubber, valve seals, pipeline pigs) and vee packing systems.

What is POLYKRAN® technology?

A proprietary technology of elastomeric compounds with aramid microfibres distributed throughout the rubber matrix. It increases resistance to extrusion, wear and permanent deformation under HPHT and sour-gas conditions. It is formulated on different bases: NEKLON® on NBR, H-NEKLON® on HNBR and VIKLON® on FKM.

Do you manufacture custom or reverse-engineered components?

Yes. We design and manufacture from a physical sample, a drawing or a technical specification, with no catalogue minimums. When there is no drawing or reference sample, we reproduce the component through reverse engineering from the original part.

Which materials do you work with?

NBR, HNBR, XNBR, FKM (Viton®), neoprene, polyurethane and EPDM, plus the aramid-reinforced POLYKRAN® compounds. Selection depends on pressure, temperature, contact fluid and the type of movement of the component.

Do you serve operations outside Colombia?

We are based in Bogotá and serve Oil & Gas operations in Colombia and the region. We evaluate export inquiries: send us the component specifications and the operating conditions.
