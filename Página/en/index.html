FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of Oil & Gas seals do you manufacture?
We manufacture elastomeric seals for wireline and slickline (inner/outer seals, stuffing box, pack off, oil saver), workover and well control (BOP, packer elements, swab cups), flowline and pipeline, and vee packing systems.
What is POLYKRAN® technology?
It is our proprietary technology of elastomeric compounds reinforced with aramid pulp, increasing resistance to extrusion, wear and deformation under HPHT and sour-gas conditions. It includes the NEKLON®, H-NEKLON® and VIKLON® families.
Do you produce custom or reverse-engineered seals?
Yes. We design and manufacture from a sample, drawing or technical specification, with no catalog minimums, and we reproduce components without original drawings through reverse engineering.
Which elastomer materials do you offer?
We work with NBR, HNBR, XNBR, FKM (Viton®), neoprene, polyurethane, EPDM and our aramid-reinforced POLYKRAN® compounds.
Do you serve international clients?
We are based in Bogotá, Colombia, serving Oil & Gas operations in Colombia and the region. We welcome export inquiries — send us your specifications and we'll reply within 24 hours.

Need a custom sealing solution?

Contact us with your technical specifications. We respond in less than 24 hours.
