Elastomeric Seals for Sour Gas Wells (H₂S and CO₂)

Sour service (H₂S and CO₂) is one of the most demanding conditions for an elastomeric component: continuous or cyclic exposure to acid gas, combined with differential pressure and temperature, is one of the most frequent causes of premature seal failure when the material isn't selected correctly. This guide explains which elastomers resist sour gas and what variables to evaluate in each case.

Recommended materials for H₂S

Of the general-purpose elastomers used in Oil & Gas, only two offer real H₂S compatibility according to their compound data sheet:

  • HNBR: recommended for H₂S — range of -40 °C to +150 °C (peaks up to +160 °C), very good compression set and abrasion resistance. It's the reference material in most critical wireline, workover and BOP applications in sour environments.
  • FKM (Viton®): recommended for H₂S — range of -20 °C to +200 °C (peaks up to +230 °C), also compatible with aromatic solvents. Preferred when service temperature exceeds the practical range of HNBR.
  • PU (Polyurethane): limited H₂S compatibility — used when abrasion resistance (sand, solids) outweighs acid gas exposure, evaluated case by case.
Not recommended in H₂S

NBR, XNBR, Neoprene (CR) and EPDM are not recommended in the presence of H₂S according to their reference chemical compatibility — using them in sour wells exposes the component to premature failure risk from compound degradation.

When a conventional elastomer isn't enough

In HPHT wells with severe H₂S, chemical resistance alone doesn't always guarantee the required service life — combined differential pressure and temperature accelerate extrusion and compression set even in HNBR or FKM. In these cases, Polymers Seals Solutions recommends POLYKRAN® compounds (aramid-pulp-reinforced) on the same elastomeric base: the internal reinforcement network improves extrusion resistance and dimensional stability under load, without changing the base elastomer's chemical compatibility.

Examples already applied in our catalog: BOP Seals in severe sour environments (HNBR or FKM), Packer Elements in HPHT wells with severe H₂S (FKM or aramid-reinforced HNBR), and Aramid Fiber Vee Packing for stuffing boxes in HPHT conditions where conventional vee packing shows premature extrusion.

Variables we evaluate before recommending a material

Correct selection for sour service doesn't depend on H₂S alone — it depends on the real combination of well conditions:

  • Temperature: maximum continuous service and transient peaks
  • Pressure: maximum differential and continuous working pressure
  • Acid gas concentration: type and proportion of H₂S and CO₂
  • Abrasive solids: presence of sand or other abrasives in the fluid
  • Movement: speed and type (static, reciprocating, rotary)
  • Acceptable replacement frequency: based on intervention criticality
