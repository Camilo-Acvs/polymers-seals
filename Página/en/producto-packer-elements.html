Workover & Well Control

Packer Elements

Elastomeric sealing elements for downhole packers. When set, they compress axially and expand radially against the casing, creating an annular seal that withstands several thousand PSI over years of service. They are completion tools installed inside the casing to isolate reservoir zones.

Request a Quote WhatsApp

Specifications

  • Function: Zonal isolation via annular seal against casing
  • Seal type: Long-duration high-pressure static
  • Working pressure: 3,000 – 15,000 PSI per design
  • Temperature: Up to 200°C per material
  • Materials: NBR · HNBR · FKM · POLYKRAN®

Applications

Permanent and retrievable packers, bridge plugs, cement retainers, zonal isolation in production and injection.

Material recommendation

Selection by temperature, differential pressure, fluid and sour gases. For HPHT with severe H₂S, FKM (-20°C to +200°C, peaks +230°C, H₂S recommended) or aramid-reinforced HNBR (-40°C to +150°C, peaks +160°C, H₂S recommended).
