Workover & Well Control

Packer Cups

Cup-shaped elements that create a directional seal by expansion under differential pressure. They seal passively: when pressure acts on the open face, the lip expands against the tubing or casing wall. Unlike conventional packer elements, which are activated by mechanical compression, this seal is purely passive.

Specifications

  • Function: Temporary directional seal in tubing or casing
  • Seal type: Differential-pressure directional dynamic
  • Working pressure: 500 – 5,000 PSI per configuration
  • Orientation: Upward, downward or bidirectional (double cup)
  • Materials: NBR · HNBR · PU · FKM

Applications

Swabbing, tubing integrity tests, selective cementing, chemical stimulation, cleaning and light workover.

Material recommendation

3%–6% interference vs the ID. For sand, PU is recommended (-40°C to +100°C, limited H₂S compatibility). Verify orientation according to the differential pressure.
