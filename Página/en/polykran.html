Advanced elastomeric materials technology to improve performance in Oil & Gas applications under extreme conditions.

POLYKRAN® is a materials technology developed by Polymers Seals Solutions to improve the performance of elastomeric components exposed to severe operating conditions, including high pressures, extreme temperatures, friction, wear and exposure to hydrocarbons, H₂S and aggressive chemical media.

THE TECHNOLOGY

A reinforced internal structure

Its development combines high-performance elastomers with high-strength aramid microfibers, incorporated and distributed within the elastomeric matrix to generate a reinforced internal structure, able to increase the mechanical stability of the material without compromising the elasticity the sealing system requires to work.

THE REINFORCEMENT

High-performance aramid (Kevlar®) for greater mechanical and extrusion resistance

Internal reinforcement

An internal reinforcing element in the matrix

The aramid (Kevlar®) microfibers act as an internal reinforcing element within the elastomeric matrix, providing high mechanical strength, thermal stability and greater capacity to withstand compression, shear and deformation stresses.

Under high pressure

Spreads the stresses and holds back extrusion

Under high-pressure conditions, this reinforced structure helps distribute mechanical stresses across the matrix, restrain excessive deformation and increase the material's resistance to extrusion and mechanical damage.

The balance

Keeps its elasticity and its sealing force

At the same time, the elastomeric matrix keeps the elasticity, recovery capacity and conformability needed to adapt to the contact surfaces, compress during installation and hold the sealing force in service, achieving a balance between structural stiffness, mechanical strength and elastomeric behavior.

THE THREE VARIANTS

NEKLON®, H-NEKLON® and VIKLON®

Same aramid reinforcement. What changes is the base rubber.

Base: Nitrile · NBR
NEKLON®
Thermal range−30 to +120 °C

Nitrile rubber (NBR) with the aramid reinforcement. Resistant to mineral oils and hydrocarbons; good resistance to extrusion, shear and wear.

Application
Vee packing, stuffing boxes, swab cups and dynamic seals in sweet crude and general service.
Base: Hydrogenated nitrile · HNBR
H-NEKLON®
Thermal range−40 to +160 °C

Hydrogenated nitrile rubber (HNBR) with the aramid reinforcement. Handles more temperature and is compatible with sour gas (H₂S), where NBR does not reach.

Application
HPHT vee packing, BOP seals, packer elements, stripper rubbers and wireline seals in high pressure and temperature.
Base: FKM · Viton®
VIKLON®
Thermal range−20 to +200 °C

Fluorocarbon rubber (FKM / Viton®) with the aramid reinforcement. The base with the most chemical and thermal resistance: aromatic solvents, severe H₂S and CO₂.

Application
Seals in HPHT wells with aggressive chemistry, high-demand BOP seals and critical completion.

MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing process

We formulate and manufacture every seal in-house, from a physical sample or a drawing.

1

Formulation

The compound is defined: base elastomer and aramid microfiber content, according to the well's service conditions.

2

Mixing

The aramid microfibers are incorporated and distributed within the elastomeric matrix until they are spread through the whole mass.

3

Molding and curing

The compound is molded to the seal geometry and cured under controlled pressure and temperature.

4

Inspection and dispatch

Dimensions and hardness are checked part by part before the order ships.
