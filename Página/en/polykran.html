TECHNICAL CONCEPT

Three-dimensional reinforcement network

POLYKRAN® is an advanced elastomeric compound technology developed by Polymers Seals Solutions, based on the integration of aramid pulp into high-performance rubber matrices. Its fundamental principle consists of dispersing highly fibrillated aramid microfibrils within the elastomeric matrix during the mixing process, creating a three-dimensional reinforcement network that significantly modifies the mechanical and thermal properties of the resulting compound.


Unlike conventional reinforcements with carbon black or silica, the aramid pulp acts as an internal structural element, distributing loads more efficiently and limiting material deformation under high pressure and temperature conditions.

TECHNICAL BENEFITS

Beyond the conventional elastomer

Extrusion Resistance

The aramid microfibril network acts as a structural barrier, preventing the elastomer from yielding through metal tolerances under high differential pressure. Primary failure mode eliminated.

Extended Service Life

Structural reinforcement reduces the rate of wear and permanent deformation in service, extending the component replacement interval and reducing total operating cost through lower intervention frequency.

Superior Mechanical Strength

The incorporation of aramid pulp increases the compound modulus and tensile strength, improving behavior under cyclic loading and high-speed reciprocating movement conditions.

Dimensional Stability

Lower permanent deformation under sustained high-temperature loading, ensuring the component maintains its geometry and sealing preload throughout its entire service life.

AVAILABLE VARIANTS

Three elastomer bases, one reinforcement

All three variants share the aramid-pulp reinforcement; what changes is the base elastomer. The variant is selected according to the well's fluid, temperature and chemistry.

Base: Nitrile · NBR
NEKLON®
Temperature range −40 °C / +120 °C

NBR (nitrile) compound reinforced with aramid pulp. Excellent resistance to mineral oils and hydrocarbons, plus the extrusion and wear resistance provided by the aramid network. The general-service option for sweet crude.

Typical applications

Vee packing, stuffing boxes, swab cups and dynamic seals in standard operating conditions.

Most requested
Base: Hydrogenated nitrile · HNBR
H-NEKLON®
Temperature range −40 °C / +150 °C

HNBR (hydrogenated nitrile) compound reinforced with aramid pulp. Adds superior thermal resistance and sour-gas (H₂S) compatibility to the extrusion resistance, for demanding HPHT conditions.

Typical applications

HPHT vee packing, BOP seals, packer elements, stripper rubbers and wireline in high-pressure, high-temperature and sour-gas environments.

Base: Viton · FKM
VIKLON®
Temperature range −20 °C / +200 °C

FKM (Viton®) compound reinforced with aramid pulp. The portfolio's maximum chemical and thermal resistance: practically inert to most aggressive fluids, aromatic solvents, severe H₂S and CO₂.

Typical applications

Seals in HPHT wells with aggressive chemistry, high-performance BOP seals, critical completion and extreme service conditions.
