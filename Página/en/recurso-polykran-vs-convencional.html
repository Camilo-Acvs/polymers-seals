POLYKRAN® vs. Conventional Elastomer: When Aramid Reinforcement Is Worth It

POLYKRAN® doesn't replace material selection — it complements it. It's an aramid-pulp reinforcement technology applied on top of an elastomeric base (NBR, HNBR, FKM) when that already well-chosen elastomer still doesn't offer enough extrusion resistance or service life under the well's conditions. This guide explains exactly what changes and when the extra cost is justified.

What it is, technically

POLYKRAN® disperses highly fibrillated aramid pulp microfibrils within the elastomeric matrix during mixing, creating a three-dimensional reinforcement network. Unlike conventional reinforcement with carbon black or silica, aramid pulp acts as an internal structural element that distributes load more efficiently and limits compound deformation under high pressure and temperature.

  • Fiber tensile strength: ~3,000 MPa
  • Modulus of elasticity: ~70 – 125 GPa
  • Decomposition temperature: above 500 °C
  • Flame behavior: doesn't melt, chars

What changes versus the unreinforced elastomer

  • Behavior under high differential pressure: standard elastomer tends to extrude; POLYKRAN® resists extrusion through its internal fiber network.
  • Deformation at high temperature: standard shows greater compression set; POLYKRAN® has lower permanent deformation.
  • Stuffing box service life: POLYKRAN® offers a longer replacement interval than the reference elastomer.
  • Behavior with sand and abrasives: standard suffers accelerated wear; POLYKRAN® has greater wear resistance.
  • Dimensional integrity in service: moderate in standard, high in POLYKRAN®.

The three variants

The three differ by their level of aramid pulp incorporation, and each can be formulated on different elastomeric bases depending on the application:

  • NEKLON®: standard applications requiring more mechanical and extrusion resistance than an unreinforced elastomer — vee packing, stuffing boxes, swab cups in standard conditions.
  • H-NEKLON®: higher aramid concentration for more demanding conditions — HPHT vee packing, BOP seals, packer elements, stripper rubbers, wireline seals in high pressure and temperature.
  • VIKLON®: maximum reinforcement, for the most extreme conditions — HPHT wells with severe H₂S/CO₂, high-demand BOP seals, critical completion.
Technical note

Numeric temperature, hardness and tensile values for each POLYKRAN® variant are still under laboratory characterization — they're updated as testing is completed. For critical application selection, our technical team evaluates each specific case.

When it's NOT needed

If the well's pressure, temperature and chemical exposure are within the comfortable range of the base elastomer (for example, standard HNBR in a moderate-pressure well without sand), aramid reinforcement doesn't add a benefit proportional to the extra cost. POLYKRAN® is justified when the observed or expected failure mode is specifically extrusion, abrasive wear or permanent deformation under load — not as a generic "better material" swap.
