Workover & Well Control

Rod Wipers

Wiping elements that remove well fluids, mud, paraffin and solids from the outside of the rod or pipe as it exits the well, protecting internal seals from contamination and reducing spills.

Request a Quote WhatsApp

Specifications

  • Function: External cleaning of moving rod or pipe
  • Seal type: Radial-contact dynamic
  • Working pressure: Low – medium surface pressure
  • Movement: Continuous reciprocating
  • Materials: NBR · HNBR · PU

Applications

Workover, beam-pump equipment, heads with rod or pipe movement, swabbing units.

Material recommendation

For sand or paraffin, PU or an aramid-reinforced compound is recommended. Verify rod or pipe diameter for correct selection.
